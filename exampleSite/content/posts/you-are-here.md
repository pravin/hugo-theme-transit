At the top of every article is a strip that shows where it sits on each of its lines: the older station before it, and the newer one after.

It is the same idea as *related posts*, but readers can see why two posts are related, and they can ride a single topic from its first post to its last.

> A good sign tells you where you are, where you can go, and nothing else.

At the end of the article, *Change here for* lists every line the post is on, so readers can jump to a whole topic.
