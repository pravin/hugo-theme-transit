---
title: "Writing Good Tags"
date: 2024-02-18
description: "Fewer, broader tags make a clearer map."
tags: ["navigation", "markdown", "text"]
---

Every tag can become a line, so tags shape the map. A few broad tags give long lines with plenty of interchanges. Dozens of one-off tags give short stubs.

1. Reuse tags rather than inventing near-synonyms.
2. Give each post two or three tags.
3. Let rare tags drop off the map. They still have their own pages.
